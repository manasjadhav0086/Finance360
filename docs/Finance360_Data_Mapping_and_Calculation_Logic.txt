====================================================================================================
Finance360 - Data Architecture, BigQuery Mapping & Calculation Logic Reference
====================================================================================================

This document provides a comprehensive, end-to-end technical reference for Finance360.
It details the origin of every metric, the BigQuery views and tables queried, the filtering
mechanisms, and the exact computational formulas used across all dashboard tabs.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

3. GLOBAL FILTER & SCOPING RESOLUTION ENGINE
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The frontend scoping engine (`getScopedMetric` in Dashboard.jsx) routes queries through the following hierarchy:

1. Time Dimensions:
   - If `Month` is selected (e.g., 'Apr', 'May', 'ALL'):
     Pulls from `monthlyCompany`, `monthlySegment`, or `monthlyBu` generated from `HeatMap_Monthly`,
     `capex_view`, `collection_view`, `enrollment_view`, and `headcount`.
   - If `Quarter` is selected ('Q1', 'Q2', 'Q3', 'Q4', or 'Total'):
     Pulls from the quarterly aggregation tree generated from `pnldisqoq_view`.

2. Organizational Hierarchy:
   - If `bu` is selected       --> Resolves DATA.bu[bu][fy][scenario][quarter]
   - Else if `segment` is selected --> Resolves DATA.segment[segment][fy][scenario][quarter]
   - Else (All Company)       --> Resolves DATA.company[fy][scenario][quarter]

3. Scenario Selection:
   - `ACT` --> Actual financial performance.
   - `BUD` --> Budget / target allocation.

4. TAB-BY-TAB DATA SOURCES & CALCULATION LOGIC
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

====================================================================================================
TAB 1: EXECUTIVE 360° (Executive360Tab.jsx)
====================================================================================================

A. TOP PULSE METRIC TILES (6 Core KPI Cards):
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Net Revenue:
   - Primary Source: Finance_View.pnldisqoq_view (metric = 'Revenue')
   - Formula: Revenue_ACT
   - Units: ₹ Mn
   - Target: Revenue_BUD | YoY: Revenue_ACT(Current FY) - Revenue_ACT(Prior FY)

2. Gross Profit & Margin:
   - Primary Source: Finance_View.pnldisqoq_view (metric = 'Gross Profit' or 'Revenue' - 'Variable Cost')
   - Formula: Gross Profit = Revenue - Variable Cost
   - Formula: Gross Margin % = (Gross Profit / Revenue) * 100
   - YoY Comparison: Current Period GM% - Prior Year GM%

3. EBITDA (Earnings):
   - Primary Source: Finance_View.pnldisqoq_view (metric = 'EBITDA')
   - Formula: EBITDA_ACT
   - Formula: EBITDA Margin % = (EBITDA / Revenue) * 100

4. Order Intake & Pipeline:
   - Primary Source: Finance_View.orderintake_view and Finance_View.OrderBizWise
   - Formula: Sum(Order Intake Amount)
   - Formula: Book-to-Bill Ratio = Order Intake / Revenue (Ratio >= 1.0x indicates expansion)

5. Operating Cost (Opex):
   - Primary Source: Finance_View.pnldisqoq_view (metric = 'Total Opex')
   - Formula: Total Opex_ACT
   - Formula: Cost Intensity % = (Total Opex / Revenue) * 100

6. Operating Leverage Index:
   - Primary Source: Finance_View.pnldisqoq_view (EBITDA %, Gross Margin %)
   - Formula: Operating Leverage Index = (EBITDA % / Gross Margin %) * 100
   - Meaning: Measures how efficiently gross margin converts into operating profit (EBITDA).

B. CHARTS & VISUALIZATIONS:
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. P&L Value Creation Flow (Waterfall Bridge):
   - Source: pnldisqoq_view (Revenue, Variable Cost, People Cost, Marketing Expenses, Fixed Costs, EBITDA)
   - Step Bridge Formula: Revenue - Variable Cost = Gross Profit - People Cost - Marketing - Fixed Costs = EBITDA

2. Business Profitability Matrix:
   - Source: pnldisqoq_view (Revenue & EBITDA by Biz1_N)
   - Dual-axis Chart: Revenue (₹ Mn) on Bar Axis vs EBITDA Margin % on Line/Scatter Axis.

3. Demand & Delivery Pipeline Engine:
   - Source: pnldisqoq_view (Revenue) + OrderBizWise (Order Intake)
   - 12-quarter dual-line tracking demand inflow (Order Intake) vs delivery realization (Revenue).

4. Multi-Year Cost Composition:
   - Source: pnldisqoq_view (Variable Cost, People Cost, Marketing, Fixed Costs, EBITDA)
   - 100% Stacked Bar: (Cost Line Item / Revenue) * 100 across FY24, FY25, FY26.

5. Revenue Contribution Sunburst:
   - Source: pnldisqoq_view grouped by finance_data.business_mapping
   - Inner Circle: Business Group (Segment_N)
   - Outer Circle: Business (Biz1_N)

6. Multi-Year Plan vs Realization Bridge:
   - Source: pnldisqoq_view (Revenue & EBITDA across FY24-FY26)
   - Grouped comparison of Actual vs Budget targets.

C. BUSINESS STRATEGIC PERFORMANCE LEADERBOARD (TABLE):
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Hierarchy Columns: Business Group (Segment_N) -> Business (Biz1_N)
- Data Metrics:
  * Revenue (₹ Mn) = Actual Revenue
  * Budget Ach %   = (Actual Revenue / Budget Revenue) * 100
  * YoY Growth %   = ((Actual Revenue - Prior Year Revenue) / Prior Year Revenue) * 100
  * Gross Margin % = ((Revenue - Variable Cost) / Revenue) * 100
  * EBITDA (₹ Mn)  = Actual EBITDA
  * EBITDA %       = (EBITDA / Revenue) * 100
- Status Rule:
  * Outperforming  = Budget Ach >= 100% AND EBITDA % >= 12%
  * Attention      = Budget Ach < 85% OR EBITDA < 0
  * On Track       = Standard operational baseline

TAB 2: PERFORMANCE TAB (PerformanceTab.jsx)
====================================================================================================

A. KPI TILES:
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Revenue: Actual Revenue, Budget Ach %, YoY Variance (₹ Mn and %).
2. Variable Cost: Direct variable costs of fulfillment.
3. Gross Margin %: ((Revenue - Variable Cost) / Revenue) * 100.
4. Total Opex: Operating overhead expenditure.
5. EBITDA Contribution: Gross Profit - Sales & Marketing Expenses.
6. EBITDA: Operating earnings before non-operating items.

B. CHARTS GRID (10 High-Impact Tiles):
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Revenue Trend: Stacked bar of Revenue by Business Group across Prior Year, Budget, and Actual.
2. Revenue - 12 Quarters Trend: Stacked quarterly revenue contribution across trailing 8 to 12 quarters.
3. Biz-Wise Revenue Contribution: Horizontal stacked bar decomposing revenue from Business Group into child Businesses.
4. Fixed & Variable Cost Trend: Dual-line chart with a soft-tinted shaded area representing the cost spread/gap.
5. Order Intake Trend: Order Intake volume across Prior Year, Budget, and Current Actuals.
6. Order Intake - 12 Quarters Trend: Trailing 12-quarter demand intake trend stacked by Business Group.
7. Financial KPIs FY Comparison: Multi-year line chart: GP %, EBITDA Contribution %, EBITDA %.
8. Financial KPIs 12Q Trend: Trailing 8-12 quarters line chart: GP %, EBITDA Contribution %, EBITDA %.
9. Cost % of Revenue FY Comparison: Multi-year line trajectories for (Cost / Revenue) * 100.
10. Cost % of Revenue 12Q Trend: Trailing 8-12 quarters line trajectories of cost intensity percentages.

====================================================================================================
TAB 3: KPI SCORECARDS TAB (KPIsTab.jsx)
====================================================================================================

A. ENTERPRISE PRODUCTIVITY METRICS:
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Total Order Intake:
   - Source: orderintake_view / OrderBizWise
   - Formula: Sum(Order Intake Amount) in ₹ Mn

2. Sales Person Productivity:
   - Source: orderintake_view + finance_data.headcount (DIS_F = 'S')
   - Formula: Order Intake (in ₹ Thousands) / (Sales Headcount * Month Count)
   - Units: ₹ K / sales person / month

3. Revenue per FTE:
   - Source: pnldisqoq_view (Revenue) + finance_data.headcount (Total Headcount)
   - Formula: Revenue (in ₹ Thousands) / (Total Headcount * Month Count)
   - Units: ₹ K / FTE / month

4. Cost per FTE:
   - Source: pnldisqoq_view (Total Opex) + finance_data.headcount (Total Headcount)
   - Formula: Total Opex (in ₹ Thousands) / (Total Headcount * Month Count)
   - Units: ₹ K / FTE / month

5. People Cost per FTE:
   - Source: pnldisqoq_view (People Cost) + finance_data.headcount (Total Headcount)
   - Formula: People Cost (in ₹ Thousands) / (Total Headcount * Month Count)
   - Units: ₹ K / FTE / month

B. CONSUMER / LEARNER UNIT ECONOMICS:
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Enrolments:
   - Source: Finance_View.enrollment_view
   - Formula: Sum(Learners Count)

2. Average Cost of Acquisition (CAC):
   - Source: pnldisqoq_view (Variable Cost % or Marketing Cost / Enrolments)
   - Units: % of Revenue or ₹ per learner

3. Annualized Student Fee (ASF):
   - Source: pnldisqoq_view (Revenue) + Finance_View.enrollment_view
   - Formula: Revenue (in ₹ Thousands) / (Enrolments * Month Count)
   - Units: ₹ K / month

4. Consumer Order Intake:
   - Source: Finance_View.orderintake_view
   - Formula: Sum(Consumer Bookings) in ₹ Mn

TAB 4: OTHER METRICS TAB (OtherMetricsTab.jsx)
====================================================================================================

1. Order Intake (OI): Summed from Finance_View.orderintake_view / OrderBizWise.
2. Collections: Summed from Finance_View.collection_view (Actual vs Budget).
3. Capex: Capital expenditure data from Finance_View.capex_view.
4. Enrollments: Learner volume from Finance_View.enrollment_view.
5. Headcount: Staffing totals and Direct vs Indirect split from finance_data.headcount.

====================================================================================================
TAB 5: FINANCIAL STATEMENTS (P&L Summary, P&L DIS, P&L Trend)
====================================================================================================

1. P&L Summary (PnLSummaryTab.jsx):
   - Source: Finance_View.pnldisqoq_view
   - Hierarchy: Revenue -> Variable Cost -> Gross Profit -> People Cost -> Marketing -> Fixed Costs -> EBITDA
   - Displays: Actuals, Budget Targets, Absolute Variances, % Variances, and Prior Year Comparisons.

2. P&L DIS (PnLDISTab.jsx):
   - Source: Finance_View.pnldisqoq_view
   - Segregation of direct vs indirect shared services and distribution across businesses.

3. P&L Trend (PnLTrendTab.jsx):
   - Source: Finance_View.pnldisqoq_view + Finance_View.HeatMap_Monthly
   - Multi-period trend and seasonality trajectory.

====================================================================================================
TAB 6: LIVE MARKET NEWS (LiveNewsTab.jsx)
====================================================================================================

- Source: Google Vertex AI Search with Grounding (model: gemini-2.5-flash)
- Logic: Real-time queries for macroeconomic, IT sector, enterprise learning, and EdTech developments.

====================================================================================================
TAB 7: AI COPILOT (aiService.js / CopilotDrawer.jsx)
====================================================================================================

- Engine: Google Cloud Gemini Data Analytics Agent (geminidataanalytics.googleapis.com/v1alpha)
- Single Source of Truth Target: BigQuery view `Finance_View.pnldisqoq_view`
- Logic: Translates user natural language questions into BigQuery SQL, executes queries against
  the verified P&L view, calculates variances and driver insights, and produces chart visualizations.
